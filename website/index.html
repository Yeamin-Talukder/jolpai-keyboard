Everything you need to type Bangla and English

One keyboard for every app: WhatsApp, Messenger, Facebook, Gmail, Chrome and more.

অ

Avro phonetic (অভ্র)

Type how a word sounds: ami banglay likhi → আমি বাংলায় লিখি. Dictionary candidates, and it remembers your picks.

জ

National (জাতীয়) and প্রভাত

The Bangladesh national layout, arranged like Ridmik, and প্রভাত (Probhat) as on a computer. Hold a key for the letter in its corner.

On-device AI for English

A small neural model reads your sentence: the next word, autocorrect that picks the word that fits ("takl" → talk), emoji by meaning. On your phone only.

Spelling & grammar check

Finds spelling, punctuation and grammar mistakes in English and বাংলা, offline, with one-tap fixes.

Offline translation

English ↔ Bangla inside the keyboard. Download a ~30 MB pack once; then it works without internet.

Voice typing

Speak Bangla or English, with spoken punctuation: "comma", "দাঁড়ি", "নতুন লাইন".

Learns how you write

Bangla next words from 91 million words of open text (কেমন → আছেন), plus your own phrases, which fade when you stop using them. All on your phone.

Clipboard & snippets

Clipboard history with pins and smart categories (links, OTP codes, phone numbers). Type @addr to insert your address.

64 themes

Material You, soft pastels, gradients and dark themes like Olive night, plus your own photo themes, key fonts and sounds.

Emoji, stickers, GIFs

Emoji search in English and Bangla, skin tones, kaomoji, your own stickers and GIF search.

Gestures & trackpad

Slide on space to move the cursor, swipe Backspace to delete words, and set your own swipes.

Typing stats

Words per day, typing speed (WPM) and accuracy. Only numbers are kept, never what you type.

Four layouts, one tap apart

Switch English ↔ বাংলা (অভ্র) ↔ বাংলা (জাতীয়) ↔ বাংলা (প্রভাত) from the top bar.

An offline spelling check for বাংলা and English

Tap ✓ Check in the top bar to see what may be wrong in what you're writing, and fix it in one tap. It runs entirely on your phone.

  • Bangla spelling: look-alike letters (ি/ী, শ/ষ/স), vowel signs and word endings
  • Punctuation: spaces around marks, দাঁড়ি in Bangla, a missing full stop
  • English grammar: a/an, "i" → "I", "could of", "better then" …
  • Each mistake shows the fix plus other likely words, ranked by your own typing

Install in a minute

Most phones need the arm64-v8a APK; older 32-bit phones need armeabi-v7a.

  1. Download the APK

    Get the latest release from GitHub. If Android asks, allow installing apps from your browser.

  2. Turn it on

    Open Jolpai Keyboard and switch it on in Android's keyboard list. Everything stays on your phone.

  3. Choose it and type

    Pick Jolpai as your keyboard, then tap the language button for English, অভ্র or জাতীয়.

Coming from 0.7 or 0.8? Version 0.9 has a new app ID, so it installs next to the old one. Save a backup in the old app (Backup & restore), restore it in the new one, then uninstall the old app. Later versions update normally.

    Questions

    How do I type Bangla on Android with Jolpai Keyboard?

    Install Jolpai, turn it on in the app's two setup steps, then tap the language button to choose বাংলা (অভ্র) for phonetic typing (ami → আমি) or বাংলা (জাতীয়) for the National layout.

    Does Jolpai Keyboard support Avro phonetic typing?

    Yes. It has a built-in Avro phonetic engine using the open Avro rule table from OpenBangla, with dictionary candidates and learning of your choices. It works fully offline.

    Does it have the National (জাতীয়) / Jatiya layout? What about Probhat or Bijoy?

    Yes, the Bangladesh National layout is built in, arranged like Ridmik Keyboard. The Probhat (প্রভাত) layout is built in too: turn it on in Languages & layouts. The Bijoy layout is not included.

    Is Jolpai Keyboard safe? Does it collect what I type?

    No. It has no account, analytics, ads or crash-reporting SDK, and stores learned words and clipboard history only on your phone, in an encrypted database. Password and incognito fields are never learned. The code is open source, so you can check this yourself.

    Does Jolpai Keyboard work offline?

    Yes. Typing, suggestions, Avro, the spelling and grammar check, the clipboard and themes need no internet. Translation works offline after a one-time pack download. Only GIF search needs the internet (and checking for updates, when you tap it).

    Does Jolpai use AI? Does my text leave the phone?

    Yes, for English: a small neural next-word model (4.7 MB) runs inside the keyboard, on your phone. It reads only the sentence you are typing to suggest the next word, rank suggestions and autocorrect, pick emoji by meaning and catch wrong words like their/there in Check. Nothing is sent anywhere, and it learns nothing. You can turn it off in Suggestions → On-device AI predictions.

    Does it check Bangla spelling and grammar?

    Yes. Check finds Bangla spelling mistakes (look-alike letters, vowel signs, word endings) and punctuation such as দাঁড়ি, plus English spelling, punctuation and common grammar, all offline. It is rule-based, so it won't judge meaning or style.

    Can I translate English to Bangla inside the keyboard?

    Yes. The Translate box translates English to Bangla (and back) as you type, on your device, after a one-time download of about 30 MB.

    Which Android versions are supported?

    Android 8.0 (Oreo, API 26) and newer. Material You colours need Android 12 or newer.

    Is it free?

    Yes. It is free and open source under the Apache License 2.0, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
