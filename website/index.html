New in 0.15

Jolpai learned Bangla, and Banglish

Our own AI models, trained for Jolpai and running on your phone, plus an app you can use in বাংলা and GIFs that just work.

ক

Our own Bangla AI model

A next-word model trained for Jolpai on everyday Bangla: chats, comments, subtitles and more. The right next word is in the top 3 22.7% of the time, up from 7.7%, and Avro picks the word you mean more often (57.5% → 61.5%).

Ab

Banglish, the way you text

Write Bangla in English letters on the English keys: valob → valobashi, in your own spelling (valo or bhalo). Banglish words are no longer "corrected" into English, and a Banglish model guesses your next word.

বা

The app in বাংলা

Tap বাংলা at the top of the home page and every settings page switches at once. Simpler pages, big tiles, and a floating button to try the keyboard from anywhere.

GIF

GIFs built in

No key, no setup: trending GIFs, one-tap topics (Haha, Love, Eid Mubarak …), your recent GIFs and moving previews, powered by KLIPY. Only your GIF search words leave the phone.

😊

A quicker emoji panel

Emoji, GIF, Stickers and Kaomoji always in the bottom bar. One scrolling emoji list with a category row, and search that shows big live results right above the keys.

🔒

Still all on your phone

The new models run offline, read only the sentence you are typing and learn nothing. Turn them off any time in Suggestions & spelling.

On-device AI

Three AI models in one Bangla keyboard: English, বাংলা and Banglish

Small neural language models run inside the keyboard, on your phone. Each reads the sentence you are typing and suggests the word that fits. No internet, no server, and they learn nothing about you.

  • Emoji by meaning ("so tired" → 😴) and their / there in the spelling check.

Features

Everything you want in a Bangla keyboard

One keyboard for every app: WhatsApp, Messenger, Facebook, Gmail, Chrome and more.

অ

Avro phonetic (অভ্র)

Type how a word sounds: ami banglay likhi → আমি বাংলায় লিখি. Dictionary candidates, and it remembers your picks.

জ

National (জাতীয়) and প্রভাত

The Bangladesh national layout, arranged like Ridmik, and প্রভাত (Probhat) as on a computer. Hold a key for the letter in its corner.

On-device AI for Bangla & English

Small neural models read your sentence: the next word in English, Bangla and Banglish, Avro picking the word you mean, autocorrect that fits ("takl" → talk), emoji by meaning. On your phone only.

Spelling & grammar check

Finds spelling, punctuation and grammar mistakes in English and বাংলা, offline, with one-tap fixes.

Offline translation

English ↔ Bangla inside the keyboard. Download a ~30 MB pack once; then it works without internet.

Voice typing

Speak Bangla or English, with spoken punctuation: "comma", "দাঁড়ি", "নতুন লাইন".

Learns how you write

Bangla next words from Jolpai's own AI model and 91 million words of open text (কেমন → আছেন), plus your own phrases, which fade when you stop using them. All on your phone.

Clipboard & snippets

Clipboard history with pins and smart categories (links, OTP codes, phone numbers). Type @addr to insert your address.

143 themes

Material You, Chameleon themes in your app's colour, glowing and bouncy ones, dark themes like Olive night, plus your own photo themes, 30 key fonts and sounds.

Emoji, GIFs, stickers

Emoji, GIFs, stickers and kaomoji one tap apart. Built-in GIFs with quick topics, no key needed; emoji search in English and Bangla with big live results.

Gestures & trackpad

Hold and slide on space to move the cursor, swipe ⌫ left to delete words, swipe the top bar to undo or redo, swipe up on space for the clipboard and down for emoji, and make your own.

Typing stats

Words per day, typing speed (WPM) and accuracy. Only numbers are kept, never what you type.

𝐁

Fancy text styles

𝐁𝐨𝐥𝐝, 𝓢𝓬𝓻𝓲𝓹𝓽, Ⓒⓘⓡⓒⓛⓔ, Sᴍᴀʟʟ ᴄᴀᴘs and more for Facebook, WhatsApp and bios: restyle a selection, or type in a style.

=

Calculator and বাংলা তারিখ

Type 250*4 and tap = 1000 in the strip. Insert today's Bangla date (২৩ কার্তিক ১৪৩৩) from the editing panel or a snippet.

উৎ

Festival themes

পহেলা বৈশাখ, একুশে ফেব্রুয়ারি, বিজয় দিবস, রমজান and ঈদ themes switch on by themselves on those days (you can turn it off).

An offline spelling check for বাংলা and English

Tap ✓ Check in the top bar to see what may be wrong in what you're writing, and fix it in one tap. It runs entirely on your phone.

  • Bangla spelling: look-alike letters (ি/ী, শ/ষ/স), vowel signs and word endings
  • Punctuation: spaces around marks, দাঁড়ি in Bangla, a missing full stop
  • English grammar: a/an, "i" → "I", "could of", "better then" …
  • Each mistake shows the fix plus other likely words, ranked by your own typing

Install

Install in a minute

Most phones need the arm64-v8a APK; older 32-bit phones need armeabi-v7a.

  1. Download the APK

    Get the latest release from GitHub. If Android asks, allow installing apps from your browser.

  2. Turn it on

    Open Jolpai Keyboard and switch it on in Android's keyboard list. Everything stays on your phone.

  3. Choose it and type

    Pick Jolpai as your keyboard, then tap the language button for English, অভ্র or জাতীয়.

Coming from 0.7 or 0.8? Version 0.9 has a new app ID, so it installs next to the old one. Save a backup in the old app (Backup & restore), restore it in the new one, then uninstall the old app. Later versions update normally.

    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the best Bangla keyboard for Android?

    It depends on what you need. Jolpai is a strong choice if you want everything in one free app: Avro phonetic typing, the National (জাতীয়) and প্রভাত layouts, Banglish, its own on-device AI for Bangla and English, an offline Bangla and English spelling check, offline translation and 143 themes, with no ads and no tracking. It is open source, so anyone can check what it does with your typing.

    Is there an AI-powered Bangla keyboard?

    Yes. Jolpai runs small neural models on your phone, in English, Bangla and Banglish: they read your sentence to suggest the next word, rank suggestions, fix typos, help Avro pick the right word and pick emoji by meaning. The Bangla and Banglish models were trained for Jolpai. Nothing is sent to a server.

    Which Bangla keyboard has the best design?

    Jolpai is built for a clean, modern look: centred Bangla vowel signs without dotted circles, Material You colours, 143 themes (light, dark, gradients and your own photo), rounded keys, key fonts and smooth animations. Try the live demo at the top of this page.

    How do I type Bangla on Android with Jolpai Keyboard?

    Install Jolpai, turn it on in the app's two setup steps, then tap the language button to choose বাংলা (অভ্র) for phonetic typing (ami → আমি) or বাংলা (জাতীয়) for the National layout.

    Does Jolpai Keyboard support Avro phonetic typing?

    Yes. It has a built-in Avro phonetic engine using the open Avro rule table from OpenBangla, with dictionary candidates and learning of your choices. It works fully offline.

    Does it have the National (জাতীয়) / Jatiya layout? What about Probhat or Bijoy?

    Yes, the Bangladesh National layout is built in, arranged like Ridmik Keyboard. The Probhat (প্রভাত) layout is built in too: turn it on in Languages & layouts. The Bijoy layout is not included.

    Is Jolpai Keyboard safe? Does it collect what I type?

    No. It has no account, analytics, ads or crash-reporting SDK, and stores learned words and clipboard history only on your phone, in an encrypted database. Password and incognito fields are never learned. The code is open source, so you can check this yourself.

    Does Jolpai Keyboard work offline?

    Yes. Typing, suggestions, Avro, the spelling and grammar check, the clipboard and themes need no internet. Translation works offline after a one-time pack download. Only GIFs need the internet (and checking for updates, when you tap it).

    Does Jolpai use AI? Does my text leave the phone?

    Yes, in English, Bangla and Banglish: small neural next-word models run inside the keyboard, on your phone (English 4.7 MB, and Jolpai's own Bangla 5.9 MB and Banglish 2.8 MB models). They read only the sentence you are typing to suggest the next word, rank suggestions and autocorrect, help Avro pick the right word, pick emoji by meaning and catch wrong words like their/there in Check. Nothing is sent anywhere, and the models learn nothing. You can turn them off in Suggestions & spelling → On-device AI predictions.

    Is there a Banglish keyboard for Android?

    Yes. Jolpai offers Banglish (Bangla written in English letters, like kemon acho) on its English keyboard: it completes words in the spelling you use (valo or bhalo), never “corrects” Banglish into English, and a Banglish AI model trained for Jolpai suggests your next Banglish word. Turn it off in Suggestions & spelling → Banglish suggestions.

    Which Bangla keyboard has AI next-word prediction for Bangla?

    Jolpai has its own Bangla next-word model (5.9 MB), trained for Jolpai on everyday Bangla and running on your phone. It suggests the next word from the whole sentence in Avro, জাতীয় and প্রভাত, putting the right word in the top 3 22.7% of the time on held-out chat (7.7% with word lists alone), and helps Avro pick the word you mean.

    Can I send GIFs with a Bangla keyboard?

    Yes. Jolpai has GIFs built in, with no key or setup: open the emoji panel and tap GIF for trending GIFs, one-tap topics (Haha, Love, Eid Mubarak …), your recent GIFs and search, powered by KLIPY. Only your GIF search words are sent; you can turn GIF search off.

    What gestures does Jolpai Keyboard support?

    Hold and slide on the space bar to move the cursor (a trackpad), swipe left from Backspace to delete whole words, swipe the top bar left to undo and right to redo, swipe up on the space bar for the clipboard and down for emoji, and swipe a panel down to close it. You can also hold Enter for emoji or slide to select text, and set your own swipes, holds and double-taps in Custom gestures.

    Can I use the Jolpai app in Bangla?

    Yes. Tap বাংলা at the top of the app's home page (English | বাংলা) and every settings page switches to Bangla at once.

    Does it check Bangla spelling and grammar?

    Yes. Check finds Bangla spelling mistakes (look-alike letters, vowel signs, word endings) and punctuation such as দাঁড়ি, plus English spelling, punctuation and common grammar, all offline. It is rule-based, so it won't judge meaning or style.

    Can I translate English to Bangla inside the keyboard?

    Yes. The Translate box translates English to Bangla (and back) as you type, on your device, after a one-time download of about 30 MB.

    Which Android versions are supported?

    Android 8.0 (Oreo, API 26) and newer. Material You colours need Android 12 or newer.

    Is it free?

    Yes. It is free and open source under the Apache License 2.0, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
