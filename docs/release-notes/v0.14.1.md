## Jolpai Keyboard 0.14.1 beta: a number pad in any text box 🔢

Asked for by a Jolpai user: a dedicated number pad, for typing numbers quickly anywhere.

### Number pad
- 🔢 **Tap 123 in the top bar** to switch to the number pad in any text box: chats, notes, search, forms.
  It closes the emoji or clipboard panel if one is open, and stays highlighted while the pad is open.
  Tap it again, or the pad's **ABC** key, to go back to the letters.
- ⌨️ **Hold ?123** on the letters page for the number pad (English, অভ্র and জাতীয়).
- The pad is calculator-style: big digits 1–9 with 0 at the bottom, `+ − × ÷ , .`, backspace, space and
  Enter. Hold a digit for its Bangla form (১, ২, ৩ …).
- 🙌 **Number pad** is also a gesture action (Typing & gestures → Gestures), for any key or the top bar.
- 🔐 The 123 button stays in password fields too, since PIN boxes are often plain password fields.
- Your toolbar gets the 123 button added once; move it or take it out in **Toolbar & panels**.

### Also in the box (new look since 0.14.0)
- ✏️ **Text editing tools** (the editing button in the top bar) have a clearer layout: a cursor pad with ▲ ◀ ▶ ▼
  around a big **Select** button, Home / End and word jumps in the corners, Undo · Delete word · Redo
  below it; and a clipboard column where **Paste** is the big button and shows a peek of what it will paste
  (dots for passwords, "Image" for pictures). Copy and Cut flash ✓ when done.

Update from About → **Update**; your words and settings stay.
