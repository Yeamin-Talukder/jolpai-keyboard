## Jolpai Keyboard 0.12.0 beta: hold Enter, a better-feeling trackpad, panels your way 🫒

### New
- ⏎ **Hold Enter for emoji** (optional). Turn it on in **Typing & gestures → Gestures**. A tap on Enter
  still sends, searches or starts a new line.
- ✂️ **Hold Enter and slide to select** (optional, same place). Hold Enter, then slide left or right to
  select text from the cursor a letter at a time, or up and down for whole lines. Let go and the text
  stays selected, ready to copy or cut. With both switches on: hold still and let go for emoji, hold and
  slide to select. Neither works in password fields. Both are off until you turn them on.
- 📳 **The space-bar trackpad ticks as you move.** Hold the space bar and slide: every cursor step now
  gives a light tick (before, only changing line did). All cursor and selection slides use Android's own
  text-cursor haptic, which you can feel clearly on Pixel phones.
- 😊 **Choose where emoji and clipboard open.** In **Toolbar & panels**: *Above the keyboard* (as
  before: opens over the app, the keys stay) or *In place of the keys* (the panel takes the keys' space,
  and the keyboard doesn't grow over the app).

### Updating
- **From 0.10.0 or later:** About → **Update**, or download the APK below and open it; it installs as an
  update and keeps your data.
- **From 0.9.0:** download the APK below and open it.
- **From 0.7.0 or 0.8.0:** the app ID changed in 0.9.0, so move your data once: in the old app
  **Backup & restore → Save backup**, install 0.12.0, restore the file there, then uninstall the old app.

### Notes
- Still a **beta**, used daily on a Pixel 7 Pro. Please report problems in [Issues](https://github.com/Yeamin-Talukder/jolpai-keyboard/issues) or with **About → Report a problem**.
- Selecting by line follows the text's own lines (new lines you typed), not the way a long paragraph wraps on screen.
- Built and signed by GitHub Actions from the tagged source, with the same release key as before; checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
