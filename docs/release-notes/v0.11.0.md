## Jolpai Keyboard 0.11.0 beta: on-device AI predictions and Bangla next words 🫒

### New
- 🧠 **On-device AI for English.** A small neural next-word model (4.7 MB) now runs inside the keyboard,
  on your phone only. It reads your whole sentence to:
  - suggest the next word, even on a fresh install ("i would like to" → thank, know, see);
  - rank suggestions and autocorrect by the sentence ("i want to takl" → **talk**, not tail);
  - make the letters of likely words a little easier to hit;
  - pick emoji by meaning, not just keywords (good night → 😴, my dog is sick → 🐶);
  - let **Check** catch wrong word choices: their/there/they're, its/it's, your/you're, then/than and more.

  Measured on held-out everyday English sentences: the right next word is in the top 3 **35 %** of the
  time (0 % before, without typing history), the right word after one letter **55 %** (24 % before),
  and wrong autocorrections fell from **12.5 % to 5.5 %**. Turn it off in Suggestions → **On-device AI
  predictions**.
- 🇧🇩 **Bangla next words from the first word.** A ranked list counted from 91 million words of open
  Bangla text suggests the next word using the last one or two words (কেমন → আছেন, আমি তোমাকে →
  ভালোবাসি), also at the start of a sentence. The right word is in the top 3 18 % of the time on unseen
  news sentences (0 % before).
- 📈 **Learns your phrases.** Jolpai remembers the word you type after one word and after two, and old
  habits fade (half weight after 30 days unused), so new habits take over. Your learned words are kept
  when you update, and included in backups.
- ⌨️ **প্রভাত (Probhat) layout.** Turn it on in **Languages & layouts**. Corners show the shifted letter;
  hold a key for it.
- ✨ **Clean vowel-sign keys.** া ি ে ু ্ and the other signs sit in the middle of their keys, with no
  dotted circle, also in the key preview and hold menus.
- 🎉 **Festival themes** around Bangladeshi festival days (can be turned off), **snippet packs** (everyday
  messages, Islamic greetings), **date and Bangla calendar** snippets, and **Unicode text styles**.

### Updating
- **From 0.10.0 or 0.10.1:** About → **Update**, or download the APK below and open it; it installs as an
  update and keeps your data.
- **From 0.9.0:** download the APK below and open it.
- **From 0.7.0 or 0.8.0:** the app ID changed in 0.9.0, so move your data once: in the old app
  **Backup & restore → Save backup**, install 0.11.0, restore the file there, then uninstall the old app.
