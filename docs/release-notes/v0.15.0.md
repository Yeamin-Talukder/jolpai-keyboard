## Jolpai Keyboard 0.15.0 beta: our own Bangla & Banglish AI, the app in Bangla, built-in GIFs 🫒

A big update: Jolpai now has its **own on-device AI models for Bangla and Banglish**, the settings app speaks Bangla, GIFs work without any setup, and emoji and GIFs are much quicker to find.

### 🧠 On-device AI for Bangla and Banglish (new models, trained for Jolpai)
- **Bangla next-word model** (5.9 MB), trained for Jolpai on everyday Bangla: chat-style web sentences, YouTube and online comments, Common Voice, subtitles, Tatoeba and Wikipedia. It suggests the next word in Bangla (Avro, জাতীয় and প্রভাত) and ranks words by the whole sentence. On held-out chat, the right next word is in the top 3 suggestions **22.7%** of the time, up from 7.7% with the word lists alone.
- **Avro picks better words:** the model now really decides which word space writes, not just how close it is to your spelling. On 9,579 real test words the intended word goes in **61.5%** of the time, up from 57.5% (58.3% with AI predictions off, up from 53.4%).
- **Banglish, Bangla in English letters, on the English keyboard:** type *valob* and get *valobashi*, in the spelling you use (*valo* or *bhalo*: sounds-alike spellings match). 16,663 Banglish spellings from BanglaTLit. Banglish words like *kmn* and *tmr* are no longer autocorrected into English (21% → 6% of Banglish words changed), and the spelling check leaves them alone. The right word after 2 letters: **35%**, up from 1%.
- **Banglish next words:** a separate **Banglish model** (2.8 MB), trained only on real Banglish (BanglaTLit, YouTube comments and more), guesses your next Banglish word: right in the top 3 **25.5%** of the time, where there were none before.
- 🔒 Everything runs **on your phone**, with no internet. Turn them off any time: **Suggestions & spelling → On-device AI predictions / Banglish suggestions**.
- The download is about 8 MB bigger because of the models (38 MB for most phones).

### The app in Bangla · অ্যাপ এখন বাংলায়
- 🌐 Tap **বাংলা** at the top of the home page (**English | বাংলা**) and every page switches at once. Numbers show in Bangla digits.
- The switch is also on the first welcome page, and a phone set to Bangla starts in Bangla.
- Only the app's pages change; the keyboard's own panels stay in English for now.

### Simpler settings
- 🏠 A cleaner home page: four big tiles for what people change most (Languages, Themes, Sound & vibration, Size & layout), then three short groups.
- ✂️ Shorter, plainer descriptions everywhere, and quick choice chips instead of long lists of options (vibration, sound style, one-handed, split keyboard, clipboard keep time, voice language …). No option was removed.
- 🎨 **Themes** is now a grid of theme cards, with the filters (All, Dark, Light, Chameleon, Festivals, Yours …) pinned at the top while you scroll.
- ⌨️ A floating **Try keyboard** button on every page opens a box above the keyboard with text, number, phone, email and password fields, so you can try a change straight away.
- Some pages have new names: Quick setup → **Easy setup**, Toolbar & panels → **Top bar & panels**, Suggestions → **Suggestions & spelling**. Report a problem moved to **Privacy & help** on the home page.

### GIFs built in
- 🎞️ GIFs work with **no API key**: trending GIFs and search, **powered by KLIPY**.
- Quick topics so most GIFs need no typing: 🔥 Trending, 🕘 Recent (GIFs you sent), Haha, Love, Hello, Thank you, Eid Mubarak, Good morning, Good night and more.
- Previews **move**, and GIFs keep their own shape.
- 🔒 Only your GIF search words go to KLIPY (with your country and a random id made on your phone), never what you type in apps. GIF search can be turned off in **Emoji, GIFs & stickers**; your own GIPHY key still works if you prefer GIPHY.

### A new emoji panel
- 😊 **Emoji · GIF · Stickers · Kaomoji** are always in the bottom bar: GIFs are one tap away. The panel opens on the kind you used last.
- All emoji scroll in one list with a category row on top: tap to jump, the row follows as you scroll.
- 🔍 **Search** now shows a search bar with large live results right above the keys while you type, for emoji and for GIFs.

### Fixes
- 🔢 Number and phone boxes open the number pad in apps built with Jetpack Compose too (Jolpai's own settings among them), where the keyboard used to stay on the letters.
- The website's sitemap had two broken addresses; fixed.

Update from About → **Update**; your words and settings stay.
