## Jolpai Keyboard 0.8.0 beta: check your writing, type faster 🫒

Installs as an update over 0.7.0 (same signing key). Your words, settings and themes stay.

### Download: which APK do I need?
| Your phone | File |
|---|---|
| **Most phones from 2017 onward (64-bit)** | `jolpai-keyboard-0.8.0-arm64-v8a.apk` ⬅ pick this if unsure |
| Older 32-bit phones | `jolpai-keyboard-0.8.0-armeabi-v7a.apk` |
| Emulators / Chromebooks (Intel) | `…-x86_64.apk` or `…-x86.apk` |

Needs **Android 8.0 or newer**.

### New
- ✓ **Check**: a new top-bar tool that finds spelling, punctuation and grammar mistakes in English **and বাংলা**, offline. Fix one, fix all, ignore, or add the word to your dictionary
- 🌐 **Translator fixes**: mistakes in the translate box show as tap-to-fix chips; nothing changes on its own. Cut, copy and paste now work inside the box
- 🎯 **More accurate English autocorrect**: better at the mistakes people really make (doubled letters, vowels, letters that sound alike), no longer turns typos into names, and leaves the word alone when two fixes are equally likely. Correct words are still never changed
- 🇧🇩 **Better Bangla suggestions**: common words come first, and Bangla spelling fixes pick the likelier word
- 📊 **Typing stats**: words per day, English vs বাংলা, and your **typing speed** (WPM, best burst, accuracy). Counts only, never what you type
- 😊 Emoji and clipboard open **above** the top bar, so the keys stay put; both pages redesigned
- 👇 **Swipe down** to close Translate, Check, emoji and clipboard
- 🔤 **Key fonts** (Hind Siliguri, Tiro Bangla, Galada, Atma), 🔊 **sound packs** (soft, typewriter, bubble, wood), and a **split keyboard** for landscape or always
- ✨ On phones with on-device Gemini Nano, an **AI** button in Check suggests a proofread of what you wrote (runs on the phone)
