## Jolpai Keyboard 0.14.0 beta: feels like your old keyboard, with Chameleon themes 🦎

### Switching from Ridmik or Gboard gets easy
- 👋 **A friendly first start**: a short welcome, in Bangla and English, asks which keyboard you used before,
  your style and Bangla layouts, and helps you turn Jolpai on. Change your answers any time in
  **Typing → Quick setup** ("Set up like my old keyboard": Ridmik or Gboard).
- 🎛️ **Three keyboard styles**:
  - **Classic** (like Ridmik): slide the space bar (`◂ English ▸`) to change language, symbol hints on the
    letters (hold `a` for @), emoji and clipboard open in place of the keys.
  - **Simple** (like Gboard): translate, clipboard and voice in the top bar, and the comma before the emoji key.
  - **Modern** (Jolpai's own): every tool in the top bar; emoji and clipboard open above the keys.
- ⌨️ **জাতীয় types exactly like Ridmik**: every key types what is on it. Checked key by key against Ridmik on a
  phone, 34 tricky cases (ক্ষ, জ্ঞ, reph, কো typed two ways, a vowel sign before its letter …), all the same.
  If you liked Jolpai tidying vowel signs for you, turn on **Typing & gestures → Fix vowel signs automatically**.
- 😊 **One tap opens, one tap closes** emoji and the clipboard, from the 😊 key, the top bar or a gesture.

### A smarter spelling check
- Bangla written in English letters (`kemon acho? ami valo achi`) is left alone instead of being "corrected"
  to English (kemon → lemon), in Check and in autocorrect.
- জাতীয় words typed key by key are understood: ো typed as ে + া, or a vowel sign typed before its letter
  the Bijoy way (ি then ক).
- The জাতীয় suggestion strip now offers spelling fixes too (পরিক্ষা → পরীক্ষা, মুহুর্ত → মুহূর্ত).
- Catches more English mix-ups with the on-device AI: to/too, by/buy, here/hear, no/know, were/where.
- Opens quickly even on long messages: the words near the cursor are checked first.

### Themes and fonts
- 🦎 **Chameleon themes** take the colour of the app you're typing in (green in a green app, blue in a blue
  one), read from the app's icon on your phone. Jolpai never looks at your screen, needs no permission and
  saves nothing. More than a dozen looks: glow, mist, neon, glass, pastel, spotlight, drifting and bouncy.
- 🎨 **143 built-in themes**, grouped by kind with filter chips: cool dark and ambient glows, themes that follow
  the time of day, drifting glows, gentle key-press effects (ink, backlight, floating letters, sparkles …) and
  **bouncy keys** that squish like rubber or jelly. Edit any of them and your copy keeps changing by itself.
- 🔤 **30 key fonts, or your own**: Bangla (Kalpurush, Baloo Da 2, Noto Serif Bengali …), stylish Bangla
  (Galada, Alkatra …), English and stylish English (Poppins, Dancing Script, Lobster …), a different font for
  Bangla and English if you like, or any font file from your phone. Key fonts have their own page under
  **Look & feel**.
- ◌ **No dotted circle** on vowel-sign keys (ি া ে) in any font.
- **Translate and Check stay readable** on every theme, including glass and glow ones.

### Also
- ↩️ **Undo and Redo that work in every app**: swipe the top bar left or right; a pill fills to show the action
  is ready, and you can slide back to cancel.
- 🐞 **Report a bug** from the home screen (or About → Report a problem): write what happened and Jolpai opens
  a GitHub issue filled in with your phone and app details. You see exactly what is included and can leave it
  out; never your typing, words or clipboard. Needs a GitHub account.

### Updating
- **From 0.10.0 or later:** About → **Update**, or download the APK below and open it; it installs as an
  update and keeps your data.
- **From 0.9.0:** download the APK below and open it.
- **From 0.7.0 or 0.8.0:** the app ID changed in 0.9.0, so move your data once: in the old app
  **Backup & restore → Save backup**, install 0.14.0, restore the file there, then uninstall the old app.
