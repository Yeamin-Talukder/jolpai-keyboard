## Jolpai Keyboard 0.13.1 beta: the keyboard sits right on the system bar 📐

### Fixed
- 📐 **No more empty band under the keyboard** on Android 14 and older phones (first seen on a
  Redmi 13C with Xiaomi HyperOS). The keyboard added room for the navigation bar even where the
  system had already placed it above that bar, so a strip of keyboard background about one key-row
  tall showed under the space bar. Now the keys sit right on the bar. Phones on Android 15 and newer
  (e.g. Pixels) look the same as before.
- 🎨 **The bar under the keyboard takes the keyboard's colour** on those phones, instead of plain
  black, with light or dark icons to match the theme.
- 📱 **Landscape:** the keys, the floating keyboard and the minimised voice pill stay clear of the
  camera cutout and a side navigation bar.

### Updating
- **From 0.10.0 or later:** About → **Update**, or download the APK below and open it; it installs as an
  update and keeps your data.
- **From 0.9.0:** download the APK below and open it.
- **From 0.7.0 or 0.8.0:** the app ID changed in 0.9.0, so move your data once: in the old app
  **Backup & restore → Save backup**, install 0.13.1, restore the file there, then uninstall the old app.

### Download: which APK do I need?
| Your phone | File |
