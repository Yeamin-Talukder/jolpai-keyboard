How it works (Jatiya rules)

  • Shift: tap once = next letter only; double‑tap = lock.
  • Full vowels: ্ (hasanta, the g key) + kar → vowel. e.g. ্+া=আ, ্+ি=ই, ্+ে=এ.
  • ো / ৌ: type ে then া → ো; ে then ৗ → ৌ.
  • Conjuncts: consonant + ্ + consonant (ক্ষ = ক ্ ষ).
  • Kar is typed after the consonant (Unicode order): ক + ি = কি.
  • Extra letters ৎ ঃ ঁ ৳ are on the ?123 page.
  • On a PC you can also use your physical keyboard (QWERTY positions).
